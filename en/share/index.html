    📤 Share & Reports¶

    Turn your asset ledger into a shareable poster, or generate your annual spending report — save it in one click and share it on Xiaohongshu, Jike, WeChat Moments, and more.

    Asset Wall¶

    Entry: the 📤 button in the top nav, or Settings → "Share & Reports" → "Generate Asset Wall".

    The asset wall poster includes:

    • 📊 Stats on top: item count / total value / daily cost
    • 🏷️ Your top category
    • 🧩 Top 12 items by daily cost (emoji + name + daily cost)

    Tip

    The popup first previews the poster as web components (crisp and scrollable); only when you click "Save Image" is a 1080×2240 PNG (3:4 vertical) generated — perfect for Xiaohongshu, Jike, and WeChat Moments.

    Annual Report¶

    Entry: the "📊 Year Report" button at the top of the Analytics page, or Settings → "Share & Reports" → "Annual Report".

    The annual report includes:

    • 💰 Year spending / daily average / items bought
    • 🏆 Top 3 most expensive items
    • 📊 Platform split bar chart

    Pick a year

    If you select a time range on the Analytics page, the report uses that year; otherwise it defaults to the current year.

    Save & Share¶

    The preview is a web component by default (not an image). Once you're happy, click "Save Image" to render a PNG:

    • 🖥️ Desktop: click "Save Image" → pick a location in the system dialog
    • 🌐 Web: click "Save Image" → the PNG downloads directly

    The image is drawn entirely on your device — no data is uploaded, consistent with the "bulk import, yours to control" promise.
